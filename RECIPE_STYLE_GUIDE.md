# StayGrubby Recipe Style Guide

Write for a competent home cook. Preserve the food; edit the document. The source is evidence, not final prose. These conventions guide Creator contract 3, deterministic normalization and acceptance tests. They do not authorize recipe development or replace the creator's review.

## Operational rules

- Give the recipe a useful, restrained title. Use concise names for meaningful preparations and matching ingredient/method sections. A simple recipe needs one preparation, not artificial sections. Generated meaningful components must not be Untitled.
- List ingredients broadly in order of use within their component. Put preparation after the ingredient when appropriate. Include supplied water, finishing ingredients and actual allocations. Reconcile method/list references without inventing missing ingredients.
- Preserve every supplied amount, unit, preparation, per-person basis and meaningful allocation. Keep separately measured uses separate unless a future explicitly verified divided-quantity representation can preserve them exactly. Never add quantities to satisfy an estimated yield.
- Consolidate redundant unmeasured mentions within the same component. Do not combine materially different preparation or separate component allocations. Remove empty conversational “some.” Prefer concise variable-quantity wording, such as “as needed” or seasoning “to taste,” only when appropriate. An unmeasured line remains unmeasured.
- Express optionality and choices clearly. Alternatives are not simultaneous mandatory ingredients. Correct clear speech noise; when a variety remains uncertain, use an accurate broader concept instead of an invented precise variety. A supported optional heat choice may become a concise generic chile option. Do not generalize a measured or materially different ingredient out of existence.
- Write direct imperative actions with useful boundaries. Remove introductory dictation and recipe-writing narration. Name ingredients rather than “the first three ingredients.” Put a prerequisite preparation before the assembly that uses it. Keep useful source doneness cues and approximate durations.
- Use ordinary culinary terms when they describe the supplied action. Minimal connective wording is allowed; a new ingredient, substitution, technique, tool, precise procedural temperature/duration or serving/storage instruction is not licensed by style. Do not invent missing oven settings.
- Fill useful yield and active prep/cooking/elapsed metadata when the workflow supports a reasonable estimate. Prefer provided, then derived, then estimated, then unknown. Record estimates honestly; an estimate is not an exact quotation. Elapsed total accounts for parallel/inactive work and need not equal prep plus cook. Missing procedural facts remain missing even when overall metadata is estimated.
- Reuse canonical cuisine and allergen taxonomies. Recipe allergens come from ingredient evidence, remain editable/informational, and never establish absence, cross-contact safety or religious certification. Do not silently project Recipe selections onto an actual cook.
- Copy-edit the whole result: names match methods; ingredients and uses agree; order is executable; repeated entries are intentional; measured facts and options survive; all four yield/time fields have been considered. Review and explicit Save remain mandatory.

## Evidence and deliberate adaptations

These are general conventions, not copied recipe prose:

- [Virginia Cooperative Extension: How to Write a Recipe](https://www.pubs.ext.vt.edu/content/pubs_ext_vt_edu/en/FST/FST-155/FST-155.html): ingredient/use order, preparation descriptions, clear sequential directions, yield and timing. StayGrubby preserves distinct measured allocations rather than mechanically adding them into an unsupported total.
- [Ontario Home Economics Association: How to Write an Easy to Read Recipe](https://www.ohea.on.ca/blog/how-to-write-an-easy-to-read-recipe): matching component headings, order of use, preparation modifiers and staged method organization. Unlike authoring a newly tested recipe, importing does not authorize inventing unspecified temperatures.
- [Katie Webster, professional recipe developer: 5 Ways To Write Better Recipes](https://www.healthyseasonalrecipes.com/5-ways-to-write-better-recipes/): complete recipe elements, active versus total timing, clean-copy review and method/list consistency.
- [King Arthur Baking test-kitchen process](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2021/07/02/king-arthur-recipe-test-kitchen-process): distinguish a written draft from a recipe actually cooked/tested. Automated Creator acceptance is not a claim of kitchen testing or live-model quality acceptance.

The immutable original canonical input and old factual/adversarial fixtures remain regression evidence. Cookbook fixtures are synthetic unless explicitly labeled captured; evaluate semantic quality rather than requiring one exact recipe rewrite.
