# StayGrubby Community Guidelines

**DRAFT for owner review — not published, not in effect. Bracketed items need an owner decision.**

**Effective date: [publication date]**

StayGrubby is a kitchen you share with friends. These guidelines apply to everything you share: posts on The Pass, photos, captions, comments, shared Recipes, your profile name, username and photo. They are part of our [Terms of Use](https://staygrubby.com/terms). We have no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

## Cook, share, cheer

Share food you made, Recipes you love and honest opinions about your own cooking. Give Hands, ask questions and encourage people. Disagree about food kindly. Credit the cookbook, author, website or person a Recipe came from when you share it.

## Be respectful

Do not harass, bully, threaten, shame or intimidate anyone, including in comments, captions or usernames. Do not attack people for who they are, including race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability or serious illness. Do not encourage anyone to harm themselves or others.

## Keep it appropriate

No sexual or sexually suggestive content, nudity or content that sexualizes minors in any way. No graphic violence or gore. No content that promotes illegal drugs, dangerous activities or weapons. Food photos only need to show food and the people who agreed to be in them.

## Be real

Do not impersonate another person, brand or StayGrubby. Do not post spam, advertising, scams, chain messages or misleading links. Do not create accounts to get around a block or an enforcement decision.

## Respect privacy and ownership

Do not share other people's private information, such as addresses, phone numbers, email addresses or messages, without their permission. Only post photos and text you have the right to share. Do not post someone else's Recipe, photo or writing as your own.

## Food safety

Do not share content that encourages dangerous food practices, such as unsafe preservation, raw foods that are unsafe for most people, or claims that a dish treats or cures a medical condition. Allergen indicators in StayGrubby are informational only; do not tell others a dish is safe for their allergy.

## Report and block

If something breaks these guidelines, report it: use ⋯ › Report on a post, comment, shared Recipe or profile. Reports are confidential; the person you report is not told who reported them. You can block someone from a post's ⋯ menu or from their row in My Kitchen › Sous Chefs, and manage blocks in Settings › Blocked Accounts. They will not be able to see your profile or what you share, and you will not see theirs. In an emergency, contact local emergency services first.

## What happens when guidelines are broken

We review reports and act on content that breaks these guidelines. We may hide content, remove it from The Pass, limit features, or suspend or end an account. Serious or repeated violations lead to permanent removal. We aim to review reports within [24 hours]. A moderator never reviews reports about their own content. If you think we made a mistake, email [support@staygrubby.com](mailto:support@staygrubby.com).

## Changes

We may update these guidelines as StayGrubby grows. We will note the effective date above.

## Contact

Questions or safety concerns: [support@staygrubby.com](mailto:support@staygrubby.com).
