# StayGrubby Community Guidelines

These guidelines cover everything you share in StayGrubby: posts on The Pass, photos, comments, shared Recipes and your profile. StayGrubby is for people 18 and older.

## Zero tolerance

We have no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Do not post harassment, threats or hate; sexual content or nudity; graphic violence; content that promotes illegal drugs or self-harm; spam, scams or impersonation; or other people's private information, photos or writing that you do not have the right to share.

## Report and block

Use ⋯ › Report on a post, comment, shared Recipe or profile. The person you report is not told who reported them. You can block someone from a post's ⋯ menu or from their row in My Kitchen › Sous Chefs; blocked people cannot see what you share. Manage blocks in Settings › Blocked Accounts.

## What we do

We aim to review reports within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these guidelines and can suspend or remove the accounts that posted it.

## Food safety

Recipes, quantities and allergen indicators in StayGrubby are informational estimates, not medical or allergy advice. Check ingredients and labels yourself.
